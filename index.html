About

I'm an AI/ML engineer (B.Tech, CSE — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) who learns by building. I take a concept — retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflows, computer vision — and turn it into a working, end-to-end system rather than leaving it as theory.

I lean hard on AI tooling to move fast — across several models, not just one. I set the direction, review every iteration, and debug until it actually holds up under edge cases. The thinking is mine; a lot of the typing isn't, and I'm not precious about that distinction.

Most of what I build is deliberately deterministic and auditable: fraud-detection logic that can't be overridden by model judgment, evaluation pipelines that catch hallucinations before they ship, vision systems tested against their actual failure modes instead of just the happy path.

Outside of applied projects, I'm doing independent research into a RAG approach I call Root System — chunk-level spreading activation as an alternative to flat similarity retrieval. Still validating whether there's a real technical delta over prior art like HippoRAG.
